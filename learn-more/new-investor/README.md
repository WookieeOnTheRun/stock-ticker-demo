# New investor track

Welcome. This track explains, in everyday language, what the Fractional Kelly Stock Analyzer does and why. You don't
need any finance or math background. When a word might be unfamiliar it is explained where it first appears, and every
term is also in the [glossary](glossary.md).

Each page ends with a **Want to go deeper?** section: links to trusted outside sources, and to the matching page of the
[experienced investor track](../experienced-investor/README.md) if you want the formulas.

> **Educational tool, not investment advice.** The app summarizes the past and applies fixed rules to it. It cannot
> predict the future, and it doesn't know your goals, your other savings, your taxes or how much risk you can afford.
